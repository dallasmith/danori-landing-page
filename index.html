Your subs don't download anything

Your subs already know how to reply to a text. That's all they have to do.

Danori texts each sub before their task starts. They reply Y to confirm, N to decline, or R to reschedule. No app, no login, nothing to install.

Confirmed subs get reminders 2 days and 1 day before.

If someone says no, one tap puts your backup in and texts them.

Move a task and Danori offers to text every sub it affects with the new date. You decide. It sends.

Every builder gets a local Danori number based on where you work, so the texts come from a number that looks local.
