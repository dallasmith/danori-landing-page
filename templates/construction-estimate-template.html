Markup vs. margin

Markup is what you add on top of your cost. Margin is the share of the price you keep. They're not the same number, and mixing them up means making less than you planned.

Say a job costs you $10,000 and you add a 20% markup. The price is $12,000 and you keep $2,000. That $2,000 is 20% of your cost, but only 16.7% of the price. So a 20% markup is a 16.7% margin.

If you want a 20% margin, you need a 25% markup. The calculator at the top of this page works it out both ways.
