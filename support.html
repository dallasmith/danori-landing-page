Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to get started?

Create your first project, pick a template, assign your trades, and you're running. Most people are in under 10 minutes. Nothing to configure, nothing to train on.

How does this affect my subs?

Barely at all. They get a text before each task starts, reply Y to confirm, N to decline, or R to reschedule, and that's it. No app, no login, nothing to install. It works on any phone, any carrier. Your name is on every message so it never feels like spam.

How do I get started?

Download Danori from the App Store, create your account, and you're in. Try it free for 30 days, no card required.

What does it cost?

Small Operator is $99 a month or $990 a year, up to 5 active jobs. Large Operator is $149 a month or $1,490 a year, unlimited jobs. Both come with a free trial, no credit card required.

Is Danori only for GCs?

Danori is built for independent GCs, residential superintendents, small builders, and flippers. If you're running trades and jobs yourself, that's who it's for.

What makes Danori different from the tools I'm already using?

Most builders run their jobs across a group text, a calendar app, and whatever they remember on the drive in. Danori replaces that with one place where your schedule lives, your subs get confirmed automatically, and the things that need your attention are already there when you open the app.

What is a Builder Operations Assistant?

A Builder Operations Assistant runs the day-to-day coordination of a construction job: scheduling, subcontractor confirmations, and status tracking. Danori is built specifically for this.

How do I automate subcontractor confirmations?

Danori texts each sub before their task starts. They reply Y to confirm, N to decline, or R to reschedule. No app, no login. Confirmed subs get reminders 2 days and 1 day out.

What's a good app for an independent GC managing multiple jobs?

Danori is built for independent GCs, superintendents, and small builders running 1 to 10 jobs from their phone. Schedule, sub confirmations, and project health for every active job, from one dashboard.

Is Danori a Buildertrend alternative?

Not really. Buildertrend is a full construction management platform. Financials, client portals, selections, built for bigger operations with an office. Danori is built for 1 to 10 jobs, run from your phone, by the guy doing everything himself. If you need the full back-office suite, Danori isn't that.

How do I stop manually texting each sub every time something changes?

Move a task and Danori offers to text every affected sub the new date automatically. Confirmations go out before each task starts. You don't have to pick up the phone.

What can I use instead of a spreadsheet to track sub confirmations?

Danori tracks confirmation status as subs reply. You see who's confirmed, who hasn't, and who needs a backup, all in one place. No spreadsheet to update by hand.

How do independent GCs manage multiple jobs without office staff?

Danori is built for 1 to 10 jobs, run from your phone. Schedule, confirmations, and budget health for every active job, in one place. Same work, a smarter system.
