Buildertrend Alternative for Solo Builders

Updated October 2026

Buildertrend is a good product. It's also built for a different builder than you might be.

Their own site says it's "built for established residential builders and contractors running complex, high-value work," and "designed for businesses that are already growing." If you're running jobs alone, with no office staff, a lot of that is more than you need.

Danori is an iPhone app for that solo builder. It texts your subs, tracks your profit, and gives your clients a progress page, so no job lives only in your head. Here's how the two compare.

Where Buildertrend is the better pick

Pick Buildertrend if:

  • You have office staff or a bookkeeper who needs to be in the system.
  • You need QuickBooks or Xero connected.
  • You or your crew use Android.
  • You need client selections, like finishes and allowances, tracked in the software.
  • Your company is bigger than one person running the jobs.

If that's you, Danori isn't the right tool yet, and we'd rather tell you that now.

Where Danori fits

Pick Danori if:

  • You're the one doing the scheduling, the sub calls, the budget, and the client updates.
  • You're newly out on your own. You know construction cold and you're running the business side alone for the first time.
  • You want to know what needs your attention today without digging for it.
  • You want to see your numbers before the job's finished.

A good assistant doesn't hand you 300 emails. They say here are the three decisions you need to make today, and I've handled the rest. That's what Danori is built to do. Your dashboard shows what needs you today. Danori sends the sub texts and the reminders. You make the calls.

How subs work in Danori

Your subs don't change how they work. Danori texts them the schedule, and they reply to the text. No download, no login, nothing to learn. You can add a backup sub, and a sub can ask to reschedule by text.

What Danori costs

  • Small Operator: $99/month, up to 5 projects. $990/year.
  • Large Operator: $149/month, 6 or more projects. $1,490/year.
  • The first month is free.

If you cancel, it takes effect at the end of your billing period.
